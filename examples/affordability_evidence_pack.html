Affordability Evidence Pack

SKOPOS CREDIT ANALYTICS

Simulated BNPL lender · 40,000 applications · approval rate 66.7% · policy: headroom ≥ £150 and bureau score ≥ 560 · generated 2026-11-19

Demonstration on simulated data. No real lender or customer data is used.

Key findings

  1. Checks scale with the amount: 25% of applications in the £0–£250 band are verified with open banking, rising to 91% over £1000.
  2. Risk rises below the threshold: arrears are 21.6% for headroom between £0 and £150 (test cell, 185 loans) against 9.8% between £150 and £300. The fitted curve levels off from about £450.
  3. Groups with significantly worse outcomes, to review for fair value and support: Age 18-24 (approval 51.9% vs 70.8%); Age 18-24 (arrears 8.8% vs 5.7%); Income: mainly benefits (approval 52.1% vs 69.2%); Vulnerable customers (approval 60.3% vs 67.5%).
  4. The current policy sits on the efficient frontier: no tested policy gives the same approvals with fewer arrears.
  5. 50 borderline declines are listed for manual review, sampled across age bands.

2. Is the threshold where risk rises?

Outcomes below the threshold come from a 5% random test cell of applications the policy would have declined. Without a test cell, nobody can see what happens below the line.

4. What would a policy change cost?

Every combination of headroom and score threshold on a grid, scored with an arrears model trained on approved loans and the reweighted test cell. The frontier shows the lowest expected arrears achievable at each approval rate.
